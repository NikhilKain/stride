Stride — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Stride (“the app”) is a step-counter and fitness-tracking application published by Vythera (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and the choices you have. We designed Stride to keep your fitness data on your device by default.

1. Summary

  • Your step, distance, calorie and walk data stays on your device. We do not upload it to us or sell it.
  • The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics so we can improve it.
  • Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.

2. Health and fitness data

Stride reads your step count from your device’s hardware step-counter sensor and, if you grant permission, from Health Connect (steps, distance, and total calories). This data is used to display your daily activity, streaks, goals and history. It is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Health Connect access is optional. You can grant or revoke it at any time in the Health Connect settings on your device. If you revoke it, Stride falls back to the on-device step sensor.

3. Location (Premium walk tracking)

If you use the optional Premium “Walk” feature, Stride uses your device location to record the route and distance of a walk while that session is active. Routes are stored locally on your device. Map tiles are loaded from the OpenStreetMap tile servers, which receive the map area being viewed in order to serve the tiles. Location is not collected when you are not running a walk session, and it is never uploaded to us.

4. Usage analytics

The Play Store edition of Stride includes Google Analytics for Firebase. It collects pseudonymous, aggregated information such as: app opens and screen usage, session length, in-app events, app version, device model, operating-system version, a randomly-generated app-instance identifier, and a coarse region derived from your IP address. This helps us understand which features are used and where to focus development.

This data does not identify you personally, and it is not linked to your fitness data. It is processed by Google; see Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security. The open-source edition of Stride contains no analytics of any kind.

5. Purchases

Premium features are unlocked with a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we receive only whether the purchase is active. We do not receive or store your payment information.

6. Permissions we request

  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION — to count your steps.
  • Health Connect (steps, distance, calories) — optional, to read activity written by other apps.
  • ACCESS_FINE/COARSE_LOCATION, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION — optional, only for Premium walk tracking.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS, foreground-service permissions — to show the live step notification and count steps in the background.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW — optional, for the floating step “island” overlay.
  • INTERNET — for analytics, map tiles, and Play Billing.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — for the Premium purchase.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your fitness data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also export or clear your data from within the app’s settings. Analytics data is retained by Google according to your Firebase configuration; you can limit ad/analytics identifiers through your device settings.

8. Children

Stride is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
